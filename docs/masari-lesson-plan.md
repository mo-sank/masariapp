Masari Lesson Plan

_A Duolingo-style learning path where every unit unlocks a new piece of the paper-trading brokerage._

# How to read this plan

**Core idea:** Learning earns capability. Users start with a view-only paper account ("Starter Stack" of \$10,000 in paper cash, tunable) and unlock trading tools as they complete lessons. Nothing in the app uses real money.

**Anatomy of every lesson (3-4 minutes):**

| **Beat** | **Time** | **Purpose**                                     |
| -------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Hook     | ~30 sec  | A question, prediction, or surprising scenario  |
| Learn    | ~60 sec  | Swipeable micro-cards, no walls of text         |
| Do       | ~90 sec  | The hands-on activity (the heart of the lesson) |
| Check    | ~30 sec  | Quick retrieval plus an explain-it-back moment  |

Boss battles and the capstone run 5-6 minutes. A ★ marks a **constructionist lab**, where the learner builds or simulates something and learns from the outcome.

**Modality tags used below:**

\[Swipe\] micro-cards · \[Quiz\] tap-to-answer · \[Sort\] drag-and-drop or match · \[Predict\] commit to a guess, then see what happened · \[Build\] create something · \[Sim\] run a simulation · \[Detective\] analyze a real chart or headline · \[Explain\] pick or write the best one-sentence explanation

# Game layer (applies to all units)

- **XP and streaks.** Each lesson earns XP, with bonuses for first-try accuracy. Daily streaks include a **Streak Freeze**, earned by beating boss battles.
- **No hearts.** Wrong answers drop into a **Rewind queue**, a 90-second daily review of past mistakes. Mistakes become practice, not punishment.
- **Ranks.** Each unit completed earns a title: Rookie → Chart Reader → Risk Wrangler → Order Pilot → Market Thinker → Masari Pro.
- **Smart Investor Score (0-100).** A process-based score built from diversification, trade rationales, holding discipline, and performance versus the benchmark. It is not raw returns.
- **Learning League.** Weekly leagues rank by XP and Smart Investor Score, never by raw portfolio gains. This avoids rewarding reckless risk-taking.
- **Cosmetic rewards.** Themes, avatars, and chart skins unlock with ranks and badges.
- **Spaced review.** A short re-quiz appears about a week after each unit, and Form B of the placement quiz returns at the end of each unit. Both double as evaluation data.

**Guardrails across all lessons**

- Paper money only. Label everything as educational, not financial advice.
- Lessons never recommend specific securities. Real tickers appear only as neutral examples.
- Fictional scenarios are labeled "simulation." Historical replays are labeled with their dates.
- Quotes are delayed. Say so on screen.

# Unit 1: Welcome to the Market

**Unlocks:** exploring, watchlists, first buy and sell, portfolio.

**Goal:** a trade within the first session, with real understanding behind it.

## L0: Placement Quest (~4 min)

- **Hook:** "Show us what you know, and we'll skip what you don't need."
- **Do** \[Quiz\] \[Sort\] \[Explain\]: 10 adaptive questions across money basics, stock basics, and risk. The path adjusts, so confident users can skip ahead to a later unit.
- **Data:** baseline score and concept tags stored for evaluation. Form B returns as the post-test.
- **Unlocks:** home path, view-only paper account with the Starter Stack.

## L1.1: Slice the Pizza ★

- **Big idea:** a share is a small slice of ownership in a company.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "If you own 1 of 100 slices of a pizza shop, what do you get?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: ownership, why companies sell shares, what the founder gives up.
- **Do** \[Build\]: found a lemonade stand with 100 shares, then sell 20 to raise cash for a better cart. Watch ownership percentages change and weigh the trade-off.
- **Check** \[Quiz\]: who owns what after the sale?
- **Unlocks:** Explore tab, read-only.

## L1.2: The Auction Room ★

- **Big idea:** prices move when buyers and sellers disagree.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: a rare item goes up for auction, so does the price rise or fall when more buyers show up?
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: supply, demand, bid, ask.
- **Do** \[Sim\]: run a live auction with bot buyers and sellers. Shift the crowd's mood with a headline card and watch the price react.
- **Check** \[Explain\]: pick the best explanation of why a price rose.
- **Unlocks:** Watchlist (follow up to 5 stocks), delayed price tickers.

## L1.3: Anatomy of a Stock Card

- **Big idea:** every number on a stock card tells you something.
- **Hook** \[Detective\]: "Here is a real stock card. What can you spot in 20 seconds?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: ticker, price, daily change, volume, previous close.
- **Do** \[Sort\]: tap-label each part of a real card. A speed round tests recall.
- **Check** \[Quiz\]: "The price is up 3%. What does that mean for someone who bought yesterday?"
- **Unlocks:** full stock detail pages.

## L1.4: Your First Trade

- **Big idea:** a market order buys at the current price, right now.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "Ready to make your first move? Nothing real is at risk."
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: what a market order is, what a share count means, what happens after you tap Buy.
- **Do** \[Build\]: choose one of six starter stocks, state your reason with a quick chip-and-sentence **pre-trade rationale**, and place the buy. Confetti, then the portfolio appears.
- **Check** \[Explain\]: in your own words, what did you just own?
- **Unlocks:** **market buy** (starter cap, tunable, for example up to \$1,000 per stock), Portfolio tab, pre-trade rationale field.

## L1.5: Win, Lose, or Hold

- **Big idea:** profit and loss are unrealized until you sell.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "Your stock is up \$50. Did you make \$50?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: realized vs unrealized gains, percentage vs dollar change.
- **Do** \[Sim\]: fast-forward a week of price moves on your holding, then decide to sell or hold. Calculate P&L, then sort scenarios into realized or unrealized.
- **Check** \[Quiz\]: P&L math.
- **Unlocks:** **market sell**, P&L display, trade history, **post-trade reflection card** ("what did you expect, what happened?").

## B1: Opening Bell Boss (~5 min)

- **Do** \[Quiz\] \[Sim\] \[Explain\]: a gauntlet that mixes Unit 1 concepts, ending in a 90-second simulated trading day with headline cards. Buy, hold, or sell a fictional stock, then explain one decision.
- **Rewards:** Rookie rank, first Streak Freeze, badge.
- **Unlocks:** Daily Market Briefing (3-card recap of what happened today).

# Unit 2: Reading the Market

**Unlocks:** charts, indices, news, benchmark comparison, screener.

**Goal:** turn raw numbers into stories, without turning charts into fortune-telling.

## L2.1: Charts Tell Stories

- **Big idea:** a chart is a story of price over time, and time range changes the story.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "Same stock, 1 day vs 5 years. Which chart makes it look scary?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: line charts, time ranges, zooming out.
- **Do** \[Sort\]: match four anonymized charts to four headlines.
- **Check** \[Quiz\].
- **Unlocks:** chart time ranges, markers showing your own trades on the chart.

## L2.2: Candlestick Decoder ★

- **Big idea:** a candle summarizes one period: open, high, low, close.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "What happened on a day the price swung wildly but ended flat?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: reading a candle, volume bars. The lesson describes the past and does not claim to predict the future.
- **Do** \[Build\]: drag handles to draw the candle that matches a described trading day.
- **Check** \[Quiz\].
- **Unlocks:** candlestick view, volume bars.

## L2.3: Whale or Minnow?

- **Big idea:** market cap, not share price, tells you a company's size.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "A \$500 share vs a \$20 share. Which company is bigger?" (Trick question.)
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: market cap = price × shares, large/mid/small caps.
- **Do** \[Sort\]: calculate and sort companies into whales, dolphins, minnows.
- **Check** \[Quiz\].
- **Unlocks:** market-cap labels and filter in Explore.

## L2.4: The Market Scoreboard ★

- **Big idea:** an index is a scoreboard for a slice of the market.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "How do you know if 'the market' had a good day?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow, and what each represents.
- **Do** \[Build\]: build a five-company mini-index, then see how weighting methods change its path.
- **Check** \[Explain\]: why does comparing to an index matter?
- **Unlocks:** index dashboard and a **benchmark line** on the portfolio chart.

## L2.5: Headline Detective

- **Big idea:** prices react to expectations, so good news can still drop a price.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: an anonymized headline appears. Guess the price move.
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: earnings, launches, rate news, surprises.
- **Do** \[Detective\]: three historical headline-and-reaction pairs. Predict first, then reveal.
- **Check** \[Quiz\].
- **Unlocks:** news feed on stock pages.

## B2: Chart Detective Case File (~5 min)

- **Do** \[Detective\]: three mystery charts with clues. Deduce which event happened and roughly when. Score on reasoning, not just the answer.
- **Rewards:** Chart Reader rank, Streak Freeze.
- **Unlocks:** Screener v1 (size and sector filters), position cap raised from 5 to 10 (tunable).

# Unit 3: Risk, Return & Diversification

**Unlocks:** volatility meter, drawdown, portfolio X-ray, ETFs, Stress Test Lab.

**Goal:** the signature unit. Learners build portfolios, then watch them survive or fail.

## L3.1: Risk Roller Coaster ★

- **Big idea:** higher potential return usually comes with bigger swings.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "Pick a ride: calm, bumpy, or wild."
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: risk, return, volatility, in plain words.
- **Do** \[Sim\]: three hypothetical investments play out over five years in a fan of simulated paths. Choose which you would hold, then check your "stomach meter."
- **Check** \[Explain\].
- **Unlocks:** volatility meter on stock pages.

## L3.2: Shake or Steady?

- **Big idea:** drawdown is how far something falls from its peak.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "Two stocks end the year at the same price. Which one was scarier?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: daily swings, drawdown, recovery.
- **Do** \[Sort\]: using real historical data, sort stocks into calm or choppy and mark the worst drop.
- **Check** \[Quiz\].
- **Unlocks:** drawdown stat on your portfolio.

## L3.3: The Egg Basket Lab ★

- **Big idea:** diversification reduces the damage from any one company failing.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "If one company hits a scandal, how much do you lose?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: concentration, diversification, what it can and cannot do.
- **Do** \[Sim\]: run the same shock against portfolios of 1, 5, and 20 stocks and compare the damage.
- **Check** \[Explain\].
- **Unlocks:** position cap raised to 15, basic Portfolio X-ray (sector pie).

## L3.4: Who Moves Together?

- **Big idea:** diversification only works if holdings don't all move as one.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "Two airline stocks vs an airline and a grocery chain. Which pair moves together?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: sectors, correlation, hidden overlap.
- **Do** \[Sort\] \[Predict\]: drag stocks onto a sector map, then predict whether pairs move together and reveal.
- **Check** \[Quiz\].
- **Unlocks:** Sector tab, concentration warnings in X-ray.

## L3.5: Basket of Baskets ★

- **Big idea:** an ETF holds many stocks in one package, and fees matter.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "How would you own 500 companies without buying 500 stocks?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: ETFs, index funds, expense ratios.
- **Do** \[Build\]: craft your own eight-company ETF, then compare its path to a broad-market ETF.
- **Check** \[Quiz\].
- **Unlocks:** **ETF trading** (broad-market and sector ETFs).

## L3.6: Build the Fortress ★

- **Big idea:** put theory into a portfolio.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "Next you'll face a crisis. Build something that can take a hit."
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: a quick recap of risk, sectors, and ETFs.
- **Do** \[Build\]: build a 10-position paper portfolio under constraints, such as a minimum number of sectors and a maximum weight per holding. Your picks are saved for Stress Test Day.
- **Check** \[Explain\]: why did you choose this mix?
- **Unlocks:** rebalance tool.

## B3: Stress Test Day ★ (~6 min)

- **Big idea:** a portfolio is judged by how it holds up when things go wrong.
- **Do** \[Sim\]: three scenario cards hit your Fortress.
  - **Hype Pop** (fictional): a hot theme collapses, for example an AI-bubble burst. Tech-heavy portfolios tank.
  - **Time Machine** (historical replay): a real market crash, labeled with its dates.
  - **Rate Shock** (fictional): interest rates jump, and growth and bond holdings react.
- **Then** \[Build\]: see damage by position, redesign, and re-run. Earn a **Resilience Score** versus a diversified benchmark.
- **Check** \[Explain\]: "What would you change before the next crisis?"
- **Rewards:** Risk Wrangler rank, Streak Freeze.
- **Unlocks:** **Stress Test Lab**, a permanent tool to run any scenario on your live paper portfolio.

# Unit 4: Order Up

**Unlocks:** limit orders, stop-loss, position-size tool, recurring buys, goal buckets, alerts.

**Goal:** control how, when, and how much you trade.

## L4.1: Name Your Price ★

- **Big idea:** a limit order sets the price you will accept, and may not fill.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "You want to buy at \$48, but the price is \$50. What happens?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: market vs limit orders, bid/ask spread, why fills can differ.
- **Do** \[Sim\]: work a live bid/ask ladder, set limit orders at different prices, and watch which ones fill.
- **Check** \[Quiz\].
- **Unlocks:** **limit orders**, bid/ask display.

## L4.2: The Safety Net ★

- **Big idea:** a stop-loss limits downside, but it is not a guarantee.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "The stock gaps down overnight. Did your stop save you?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: stop orders, triggers, gap risk.
- **Do** \[Sim\]: replay a drop with and without a stop, including a gap day where the stop fills at a worse price.
- **Check** \[Explain\].
- **Unlocks:** **stop-loss orders**.

## L4.3: How Big a Bet? ★

- **Big idea:** how much you put in matters as much as what you pick.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "Same stock, 5% of your money or 50%. What changes?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: position sizing, concentration, risk per trade.
- **Do** \[Build\] \[Sim\]: allocate paper cash across five trade ideas with different confidence levels, then run many simulated outcomes to see which sizing survives bad streaks.
- **Check** \[Quiz\].
- **Unlocks:** position-size calculator in the order ticket, per-position cap raised.

## L4.4: Auto-Pilot ★

- **Big idea:** investing steadily over time smooths out bad timing.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "Invest everything today, or a little each month?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: dollar-cost averaging, lump sum, trade-offs.
- **Do** \[Sim\] \[Build\]: compare lump sum vs DCA on real historical paths, then schedule your own recurring paper buy.
- **Check** \[Explain\].
- **Unlocks:** **recurring buys**.

## L4.5: The Snowball and the Melt ★

- **Big idea:** time and compounding grow money, while inflation quietly shrinks it.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "Which grows more: a bigger start or a longer wait?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: compounding, time horizon, inflation, investing vs trading.
- **Do** \[Sim\]: sliders for years, contributions, return, and an inflation toggle. Build a "Future You" projection.
- **Check** \[Quiz\].
- **Unlocks:** goal buckets (label holdings short-term or long-term), snowball calculator.

## B4: Strategy Showdown (~5 min)

- **Do** \[Build\]: two personas. Maya needs money in two years, and Leo is investing for twenty. Design an order plan for each, covering order types, sizing, and recurring buys. Scored against a rubric of fit, not a single right answer.
- **Rewards:** Order Pilot rank, Streak Freeze.
- **Unlocks:** price alerts.

# Unit 5: Think Like an Investor

**Unlocks:** fundamentals, earnings calendar, valuation, dividends, bond ETFs, Behavior Insights, Pro dashboard.

**Goal:** move from reacting to prices to understanding businesses and your own habits.

## L5.1: How Companies Make Money ★

- **Big idea:** revenue, costs, and profit explain a business.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "A company sells more every year but loses money. How?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: revenue, costs, profit, margins.
- **Do** \[Build\]: run a lemonade stand's income statement and adjust prices and costs to change profit.
- **Check** \[Quiz\].
- **Unlocks:** Fundamentals tab (revenue and profit snapshot).

## L5.2: Beat or Miss

- **Big idea:** earnings matter relative to expectations.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "The company made record profit. Why did the stock fall?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: earnings reports, expectations, guidance.
- **Do** \[Predict\]: three past earnings releases. Predict beat or miss and the price reaction, then reveal.
- **Check** \[Explain\].
- **Unlocks:** earnings calendar and alerts.

## L5.3: Price Tag Detective

- **Big idea:** P/E compares price to earnings, so the same price can be cheap or expensive.
- **Hook** \[Detective\]: "Two companies, same share price. Which is the better deal?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: P/E ratio, growth vs value, limits of any single metric.
- **Do** \[Build\]: write a simple screener rule using P/E, then test what it finds.
- **Check** \[Quiz\].
- **Unlocks:** P/E and valuation filters in Screener.

## L5.4: Paycheck Stocks ★

- **Big idea:** dividends are a share of profits paid to owners.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "Can a stock pay you without you selling it?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: dividends, yield, reinvesting.
- **Do** \[Sim\]: simulate a year of dividends on a paper holding, with and without reinvesting.
- **Check** \[Quiz\].
- **Unlocks:** dividend tracking, reinvest toggle (paper).

## L5.5: The Interest-Rate See-Saw ★

- **Big idea:** when rates rise, existing bond prices generally fall.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "Rates just went up. What happens to your bond?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: bonds, yields, rates and stocks.
- **Do** \[Sim\]: an interactive see-saw, then balance a stock-and-bond mix to hit a target risk level.
- **Check** \[Explain\].
- **Unlocks:** bond ETFs, risk dial (asset-allocation slider) in X-ray.

## L5.6: Your Brain vs. the Market

- **Big idea:** common biases like FOMO, panic selling, and anchoring cost investors money.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "A stock is up 40% this week and everyone is buying. What do you do?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: FOMO, loss aversion, anchoring, herd behavior.
- **Do** \[Detective\]: three hype scenarios, then see what **your own trade journal** says about your habits, such as selling right after a dip.
- **Check** \[Explain\]: name one habit to practice.
- **Unlocks:** **Behavior Insights** (personal analytics from your trades and rationales).

## B5: Bubble Detective ★ (~6 min)

- **Do** \[Sim\] \[Detective\]: a historical bubble replay with a "hype meter." Watch it unfold and choose when you would exit, then compare against what happened and against your own habits.
- **Rewards:** Market Thinker rank, Streak Freeze.
- **Unlocks:** Pro dashboard (combined fundamentals, risk, and behavior view).

# Unit 6: Real-World Ready

**Unlocks:** after-tax view, scam shield, Launchpad Report, Seasons mode.

**Goal:** connect the sandbox to real life, safely. This content is general information, not tax or legal advice.

## L6.1: Keep More of Your Gains

- **Big idea:** taxes and fees shrink returns.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "You gained 10%. Do you keep 10%?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: short-term vs long-term gains, fees, why holding period can matter.
- **Do** \[Build\]: a net-return calculator that adds fees and an illustrative tax rate.
- **Check** \[Quiz\].
- **Unlocks:** after-tax and fee view (illustrative).

## L6.2: Spot the Scam

- **Big idea:** hype and "guaranteed returns" are red flags.
- **Hook** \[Detective\]: a message in a group chat promises easy money.
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: pump-and-dump, fake guarantees, impersonation, urgency tricks.
- **Do** \[Sort\]: swipe a stack of real-style messages into safe or scam, with feedback on each.
- **Check** \[Quiz\].
- **Unlocks:** hype meter on news items, Scam Shield badge.

## L6.3: Launchpad ★

- **Big idea:** a plan beats a hunch.
- **Hook** \[Predict\]: "If this were real money, what would you want to know first?"
- **Learn** \[Swipe\]: goals, time horizon, general info about custodial accounts and retirement accounts for teens with earned income, and how to talk with a parent or mentor.
- **Do** \[Build\]: write your one-page investing plan covering goal, time horizon, rules for buying and selling, and how you'd handle a drop.
- **Check** \[Explain\].
- **Unlocks:** **Launchpad Report** (shareable PDF to take to a parent or mentor).

## C: The Masari Challenge ★ (~6 min per round)

- **Do** \[Build\] \[Sim\]: submit your portfolio and plan, then face three randomized scenarios (one historical, two fictional). Your Smart Investor Score is computed, and you reflect on what you'd change.
- **Rewards:** Masari Pro rank, a cosmetic set, final badge.
- **Unlocks:** **Seasons mode** (periodic competitions scored by process, not raw returns), **custom scenario builder**.

# Tracking and data (for later)

Every lesson should have:

- A unique ID (as above) and concept tags for each question.
- Logged events: start, completion, per-item answers, time on task, Rewind hits.
- For ★ labs: the learner's choices (such as the portfolio they built) and the outcome they saw.
- For trades: pre-trade rationale, post-trade reflection, and holding period.
- Placement Quest Form A and Form B items, so evaluation uses equivalent but different questions.

# Open design decisions

- Mascot or guide character (name and personality).
- Exact caps and thresholds (starter limit, position limits, XP values).
- Which scenarios are fictional vs historical replays, and where real data is needed.
- Whether any units should be optional or skippable beyond the placement quiz.
